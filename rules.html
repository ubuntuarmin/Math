💸 Spending Credits

What To Do With Credits

Credits unlock more time inside game sites. You get 30 free minutes per browser session shared across all links. When time runs out, spend 50 credits to top up.

⏱️
Buy Session Time — 50 🪙 per top-up

When your session timer runs out, click the + button next to the countdown to purchase more time. The amount of extra time you get depends on your rank (see the tier table below).

  • Basic → +45 minutes per top-up
  • Silver → +60 minutes per top-up
  • Gold → +120 minutes per top-up
  • VIP → +360 minutes (6 hours!) per top-up
🚀
Unlock Higher Ranks

Credits also accumulate toward your lifetime total, which determines your tier. Higher tiers unlock more session time per purchase. Credits you earn are always tracked, even the ones you spend.

📋 Community Rules

Rules You Must Follow

The community is self-governing. Breaking these rules will result in your links being removed and your account flagged.

🚫
No Fake or Spam Links

Links that are broken, misleading, or designed purely for farming credits will be reported and removed. 3 reports by different users removes a link instantly.

⏱️
Respect Rate Limits

You can share up to 3 links per hour, write up to 10 reviews per 10 seconds, upvote up to 30 links per hour, and purchase session time up to 10 times per hour. These limits prevent abuse.

✏️
Edit Policy

You can edit your submitted link once every 24 hours. Editing does not affect your credits.

⚖️
Appeals Process

If your link is removed, you can submit an appeal. Ten community members vote on whether it should be reinstated. Voters earn +10 credits for each vote cast. You can submit up to 2 appeals per day.

🗑️
Deleting Your Link

You can delete your own link at any time but you will lose the 50 credits you earned from sharing it. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

😴
Stay Active

Accounts inactive for 37 days are flagged for deletion. Log in regularly to keep your account and credits safe.

👥
Session Capacity

Each link supports a maximum of 6 concurrent users in a session at once. If a link is full, wait for a slot to free up.

🎮 Sessions Explained

How Sessions Work

Every browser session starts with 30 free minutes shared across all links. The timer ticks down while you are inside any game site and pauses when you close the viewer.

Once the 30 minutes run out, you can top up by spending 50 credits. The extra time added depends on your rank. Link owners never use session time — they can always open their own links for free.

Session time is stored in your browser (sessionStorage). Closing the tab or opening a new browser window starts a fresh 30-minute session.
